Sushi Simple Recipe

Description

Sushi is traditionally made with medium grain white rice, although it can also be prepared with brown rice or short grain rice.

Sushi is sometimes confused with sashimi, a dish that consists of thinly sliced raw fish or occasionally meat, without sushi rice.

Today, sushi is considered one of the most popular dishes in the world.

But im gonna show you the simple recipe of sushi but rice.

Ingredients

  • newly cooked rice
  • any kind of cooking oil
  • white pepper
  • white sugar
  • 1 small piece kombu
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tsp salt

Procedure

  1. Place the newly cooked rice in a large bowl.

  2. Add the sugar, mirin, salt, white pepper, and cooking oil.

  3. Mix gently until all the ingredients are well combined.

  4. Add the kombu and let the rice rest for a few minutes.

  5. Remove the kombu, sprinkle sesame seeds, and let the rice cool before using.

Serving

Serve the sushi rice with your favorite sushi ingredients and enjoy!
