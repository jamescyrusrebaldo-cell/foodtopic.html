Dumpling simple Recipe

Description

Dumplings are a broad class of dishes that consist of pieces of cooked dough made from a variety of starchy sources, often wrapped around a filling.

The dough can be based on bread, wheat or other flours, or potatoes, and it may be filled with meat, fish, tofu, cheese, vegetables, or a combination.

Dumplings may be prepared using a variety of cooking methods and are found in many world cuisines.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup ground pork
  • 1 cup cabbage, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup carrots, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp spring onions, chopped
  • 1 tbsp garlic, minced
  • 1tbsp ginger, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • dumpling wrappers

Procedure

  1. Mix the ground pork, cabbage, carrots, spring onions, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, and white pepper in a bowl.

  2. Place a small amount of filling in the center of each dumpling wrapper.

  3. Fold and seal the edges of the wrapper.

  4. Arrange the dumplings in a steamer and steam for 15– 20 minutes.

  5. Serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce.

Serving

Serve the dumplings hot with soy sauce or your favorite dipping sauce.
